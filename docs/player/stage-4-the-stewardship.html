Stage 4 — The Stewardship

A world in your care. Stage 4 is graduation — the full game, with nothing held back and nothing left to unlock.

Your first session

  1. Create a stage-4 world — promptworld new my-village --stage stage-4 (no flag needed once you've earned it) — then start it and open promptworld ui my-village. The complete tool roster, capstone capabilities included, is yours from the first turn.
  2. Set the pace you want and step back — steward the village through your guardian instead of directing every single act yourself.
  3. There's no scenario gating this stage: an ordinary, ambient stage-4 world is unscored by design, so there's nothing to "pass" just to keep playing at this stage — this is the ladder's top rung.

What it is

By stage 4 you've learned to ask well (stage 1), to write durable instructions (stage 2), and to design capabilities (stage 3). Stage 4 is where those come together: the concept here is mastery through indirect influence at the scale of a whole world, rather than any single new skill. There's no fifth stage waiting behind it — the ambient world itself, running at whatever pace you like, is the endgame.

What you get

The complete tool roster, including the game's capstone capabilities (such as canonization — the power to formally enshrine something into a world's lasting law). Nothing is withheld here; every option is on the table, and it's on you to decide how (and how much) to use.

There's no scenario to pass

Unlike stages 1 through 3, stage 4 has no pass/fail exercise gating it — it's graduation, not another rung. Once you've earned it (by clearing stage 3's scenario), it's yours for every world you create afterward, exactly like every earlier stage.

Starting a stage-4 world

promptworld new my-village --stage stage-4

Haven't earned it yet? You can still start here on day one with --override — promptworld will tell you plainly what stages (and their concepts) you're skipping, and record the override honestly so the run stays comparable as an overridden one. There's no wrong way to play; the ladder is there to teach, not to gatekeep.

Check what you've earned any time with promptworld stages — it lists all four stages side by side, with what each teaches, what it grants, and what proves it.
