Stage 1 — The Voice

You speak, it acts. Stage 1 is where every new player starts: a conversation with your guardian, and nothing more complicated than that.

promptworld has a four-stage learning ladder (spec 046) — a way of unlocking more of the game's power as you get comfortable with the layer below it. Stage 1 is the floor everyone starts on. There's no shame in staying here for a while, and no clock pushing you off it.

What you're learning

Conversational prompting — asking your guardian well, watching what happens, and adjusting how you ask based on what you see. That's the whole skill at this stage: everything else in promptworld builds on it.

What your guardian can do here

At stage 1 your guardian has the basics: it can send a vision (a private word to one villager) or an omen (a message the whole village can hear), and it can set a watch — asking the guardian to keep an eye out for something and act (or tell you) the moment it happens, so you don't have to sit there watching yourself. That's it. No world-shaping miracles, no custom instruction file, no extra tools — just a voice you can talk to and trust.

Your instruction file is locked (on purpose)

Every world has a charter.md file — the guardian's standing instructions, the one thing about it you can permanently edit. At stage 1, that file is locked: your guardian works from a built-in starting charter (the friendly "tutor" one, unless you asked for the plain default) no matter what you write into the file. If you edit it anyway, your guardian will say so plainly next time you talk to it — never silently ignoring you. That's deliberate: stage 1 is about learning to ask well in the moment, not yet about writing durable instructions. That's stage 2's whole lesson.

How you move up

Pass the first-night scenario — keep the village alive through its first night by directing your guardian well (a vision here, an omen there, a watch set at the right moment). Passing it unlocks stage 2 — The Written Word for every world you create from then on, and the game tells you when it happens: your guardian's chronicle narrates the moment, and your status screen shows it too. If you don't pass on the first try, the run's ending page (the morgue) tells the story of what went wrong — read it like a postmortem, not a scolding.

Check what you've earned any time with promptworld stages — it lists all four stages, what each teaches and grants, and whether you've earned it yet.

Starting a stage-1 world

promptworld new my-village --stage stage-1

This is also the default — a brand new player who runs promptworld new my-village with no --stage at all lands here automatically. See getting started for the rest of the setup.
