# Brief and Assumptions

## The request (restated)

> "Do another vault on this information or a companion or extension that sweeps up some of
> the gameplay ideas as well."

This is the companion to the sibling branch on player documentation (same comparator games,
researched 2026-07-24). That branch deliberately excluded gameplay/feature material; this
branch is where those ideas go. Per the vault's isolation rule the two branches do not
cross-link — shared context is duplicated here.

## Constraints carried into the research

- **In scope:** gameplay *patterns* from the same family of games — how RimWorld, Dwarf
  Fortress, roguelikes, god games, and LLM-agent sims structure play: drama pacing, failure,
  the player's mode of control, observation vs intervention, difficulty framing.
- **Out of scope:** documentation/onboarding patterns (the sibling branch's topic); verdicts
  about what promptworld should adopt.

## Assumptions

- "A game such as this" = promptworld: a TUI ambient simulation of an LLM-agent village
  where the player mostly reads a story feed and intervenes through a divine intermediary
  (Metatron). Angles weighted accordingly: story generation, indirect/divine control,
  observation-heavy play, and how sims dial intensity.
- Comparators extend beyond the docs branch's list where the gameplay angle demands it:
  god games (Populous, Black & White, WorldBox), idle/observation games (Progress Quest),
  and the Generative Agents ("Smallville") research line.

## Open questions

- Which of these patterns fit promptworld's actual design is analysis-phase work, not
  research.
