# Difficulty Dials and Dynamic Depth

How deep games let players choose their intensity without splitting the design — and how
much the *framing* of that choice matters.

## Dynamic depth: one game, self-selected engagement level

Cogmind's stated design: "at the simplest level you can spend 15 minutes shooting robots
without worrying about details, just attaching the highest-rated parts you find," while
deeper players "examine stats and abilities, put together a focused build, hack terminals
for intel, and explore alternate areas" ([[_grounding]] § Cogmind). Depth is available on
demand rather than gated — the same run supports both engagement levels.

## Framing the dial: Rogue / Adventurer / Explorer

Cogmind's difficulty history is a documented natural experiment in naming
([[_grounding]] § Cogmind):

- Original scheme: default plus "Easy/Easier/Easiest" — retrospectively judged "me being way
  too conservative, emphasizing the 'proper' way to play, and everything else is below that."
- Result: **only ~9.5% of players used non-default modes**, partly stigma, partly
  discoverability — the settings "weren't prominent in menus, the manual, or tutorials" and
  the game silently defaulted to the hardest mode.
- Fix: rename to **Rogue / Adventurer / Explorer** ("Would you rather lower the difficulty…
  to 'easier,' or switch from Rogue to Adventurer?") and add **a difficulty selection menu
  on first startup** so every player makes an informed choice.
- Acknowledged cost: easier settings "somewhat destabilize" a tight design, giving "a less
  consistent difficulty curve."

## The same move in RimWorld: persona instead of number

RimWorld reaches the identical destination from fiction: choosing Cassandra, Phoebe, or
Randy *is* the difficulty/pacing choice — "the main mechanism used to determine game
difficulty and play style" — expressed as "who narrates your story" rather than a scale of
easy to hard ([[_grounding]] § RimWorld; detail in [[Storyteller-Driven-Pacing]]). Phoebe's
slow pacing functions as the newcomer mode without being labeled easy ([[_grounding]]).
