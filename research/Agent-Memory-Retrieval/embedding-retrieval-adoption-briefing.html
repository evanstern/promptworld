Should promptworld adopt embedding-based memory retrieval?

Rendering of Analysis-Embedding-Retrieval-Adoption.md — the verdict, the build order, the determinism rule, and what cuts against it. Every figure traces to the branch grounding.

Yes — as a read-time relevance term and a consolidation trigger. Never as a write-time replacement for the salience table.

Determinism is satisfied by one rule: embeddings are recorded at emission and replay never re-embeds. Vectors are payload data; every derived score is a pure function of recorded floats.

promptworld is one term short of the reference architecture

The canonical retrieval score (Park et al. 2023, ablation-validated in Smallville — the closest published analog) is a weighted sum of three normalized terms. promptworld already has two of them.

The cost side collapses at village scale

Per-agent memory stores (hundreds to low thousands of vectors) sit orders of magnitude below where vector-search machinery becomes necessary. Exact brute-force cosine is sub-millisecond here; a 384-dim local model embeds in ~4 ms on CPU.

Build order

Order carries the argument: (1) is the best-evidenced, cheapest win; (2) is what makes (1) earn its keep; (3) reuses a slot that already exists; (4) loses on every axis measured.

STEP 1

Embed-at-emission + relevance in selection

Record each memory's vector at emission; add the cosine term to the window score. Ablation-backed; near-free at this scale.

STEP 2

Open-ended memory text

Ship with or right after 1 — it gives the relevance term resolution. Situated where/why texts + gists/digests/omens already vary enough to start.

STEP 3

Consolidation clustering + habituation

SAGE/RecMem cheap-geometry-first patterns in the nightly digest slot, which is already licensed to reweigh.

DEFERRED

Write-time kNN salience scoring

Salience stays a deterministic control-plane signal. Neighbor-propagation from self-inserted labels is an unevaluated feedback loop.

Two guardrails the solution must carry

Reject recency-from-last-access

The reference design decays recency from a memory's last access — retrieval mutates state on read. That is hostile to a pure selection function and to byte-identical replay. Keep creation-time decay; if access-refreshing is ever wanted, accesses must become recorded events.

Instrument rank-divergence first

In a world where days repeat, relevance may just echo recency. Before the three-term score drives prompts, log the rank-divergence between it and today's salience×recency score — if the divergence is negligible, the relevance term demotes below consolidation work.

What cuts against the verdict

  • Operational surface. A local embedding model (ONNX bindings or a llama.cpp sidecar) is a new moving part in a pure-Go project; the model file becomes part of replay hygiene — old and new vectors must share a space, or re-embedding must be modeled as events.
  • Temporal structure beat dense similarity on benchmarks. Zep's temporal knowledge graph scored 63.8% vs Mem0's 49.0% on LongMemEval. promptworld's tick/subject/origin metadata is already strong temporal-relational structure — relevance complements it; metadata-aware retrieval (filter by subject, then rank by cosine) likely beats naive cosine over everything.
  • What is given up: the "one embedding pipeline scores everything" elegance. Salience and relevance stay separate signals — which is the shape of the systems that actually work.
