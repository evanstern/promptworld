# Feature Specification: wiki corpus size-budget debt — bring docs/wiki under the corpus-spec caps

**Feature Branch**: `task-165-wiki-size-budget`

**Created**: 2026-07-29

**Status**: Draft

**Input**: TASK-165 — "Wiki corpus size-budget debt: ~24 freshness findings on oversized notes/capsules"

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - The freshness gate exits 0 on main (Priority: P1)

As a session running the grounding-wiki freshness gate during any task's in-branch
re-pin, I want the gate to report only findings CAUSED by my change, so pre-existing
size-budget noise stops masking real staleness (the TASK-163 experience: 26 unrelated
findings drowning the signal).

**Why this priority**: every sweep task re-pins wiki notes; the debt taxes every lane
until it is paid.

**Independent Test**: `node <grounding-wiki>/gates/cli.mjs freshness . docs/wiki`
exits 0 on the branch (and on main after merge).

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the corpus after this change, **When** the freshness gate runs, **Then**
   it reports zero size-budget findings and zero capsule-budget findings.
2. **Given** any note that was split, **When** a reader loads the parent note,
   **Then** the parent carries a summary plus `[[child]]` links and no fact from the
   original body is lost across the parent+children set.

---

### User Story 2 - Routing still works capsule-first (Priority: P2)

As a session orienting on the corpus via CAPSULES.md, I want the two oversized
capsule descriptions rewritten under 500 chars and CAPSULES.md regenerated by script,
so whole-corpus routing stays accurate after the splits.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the rewritten descriptions, **When** `capsules.mjs` regenerates
   CAPSULES.md, **Then** the gate reports no capsule-budget finding and every new
   child note appears in the rollup.
2. **Given** INDEX.md, **When** a reader routes to a split concept, **Then** the
   index lists the new children under their parent (the corpus's existing
   parent/child idiom).

---

### Edge Cases

- A note within ~300 chars of the cap where splitting would orphan a coherent
  concept: tighten prose in place — allowed ONLY if no fact is dropped (AC "no
  content lost" is the invariant, not the mechanism).
- A note that is genuinely one indivisible catalog: mark `size_budget_exempt:
  <reason>` instead of a forced split; the reason must say why no summary-style
  split works.
- Split children MUST carry full frontmatter (description capsule ≤500 chars,
  sources, verified_against) and honest pins: the child's pin is the branch commit
  whose content was verified, never a copied stale hash.
- `docs/player/` pages pin wiki notes as sources — every touched note stales pages;
  the branch must regenerate affected pages (pr gate `player-docs-stale` blocks
  otherwise).

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: Every finding in the 2026-07-29 inventory below MUST be resolved by
  exactly one of: prose tightening (no fact dropped), summary-style split per
  docs/corpus-spec.md, or `size_budget_exempt: <reason>`.
- **FR-002**: No content lost: for every split/tightened note, all facts in the
  pre-change body remain findable in the parent+children set.
- **FR-003**: The two capsule descriptions over 500 chars (guardian-faith.md,
  guardian-instruction-surface.md) MUST be rewritten under budget without losing
  routing value.
- **FR-004**: CAPSULES.md MUST be regenerated by `capsules.mjs` (never hand-edited);
  INDEX.md updated to list new children.
- **FR-005**: All new/modified notes carry valid frontmatter and honest
  verified_against pins reachable from the branch tip.
- **FR-006**: `docs/player/` regenerated for every page the wiki changes stale;
  player-docs freshness probe exits 0 on the branch.
- **FR-007**: The grounding-wiki freshness gate exits 0 on the branch tip.

## Assumptions

- The corpus-spec's summary-style split procedure (docs/corpus-spec.md, "Note size
  budget and summary-style splits") is the authority for split mechanics.
- The pr gate does not block on this finding class (card note), so sibling sweep
  branches are unaffected until merge; after merge they inherit a quieter gate.
- Exemptions are expected to be rare (0–3 notes); a corpus full of exemptions would
  defeat the budget's purpose.
